## REALIZATION (REFLECTION)

**1. What did you discover about your own computer unit that you did not know before doing this activity?**  
I discovered that my computer has different hardware components and ports that each have their own purpose. I also learned how to check my computer's device name, system information, hardware components, and installed applications. Before this activity, I mostly used my computer without knowing much about its specifications.

**2. Which hardware component do you think matters most to how your computer performs, and why?**  
I think the CPU (processor) matters most because it processes instructions and helps the computer run programs. A good processor allows the computer to handle tasks smoothly and respond faster. I realized that the CPU works together with other components, such as RAM and storage, to improve the computer's overall performance.

**3. How might knowing your computer's hardware and software specifications help you as a future IT/CS professional?**  
Knowing my computer's specifications can help me understand what hardware and software are needed for different tasks. As a future IT/CS professional, this knowledge can help me troubleshoot problems, install compatible software, and choose appropriate hardware. It also helps me become more familiar with how computers work.

**4. What difficulties, if any, did you encounter while completing this activity, and how did you resolve them?**  
One difficulty I encountered was finding some of my computer's hardware and system information. I resolved this by using Settings, System Information, and Device Manager to locate the information I needed. I also checked the names and details carefully to make sure that my answers matched what my computer actually showed.
